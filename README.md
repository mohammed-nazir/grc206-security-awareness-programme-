## Programme at a glance

| Area | Design |
|---|---|
| Organisation | Northbridge Logistics, a fictional business with 800 employees |
| Duration | 12 months |
| Priority risks | Invoice fraud and BEC, credential compromise, customer-data mishandling |
| Audience model | Finance/procurement, warehouse/logistics, remote sales, head office, leadership and IT/security |
| Lifecycle | Assess, Plan, Deliver, Measure, Improve and Sustain |
| Measurement | Training reach, phishing behaviour, MFA coverage, practical assessments and incident trends |

## Assignment coverage

1. **Assess:** risks, audiences and baseline measures
2. **Plan:** objectives, sponsorship, ownership and role-based topics
3. **Deliver:** learning methods, onboarding, phishing simulations and no-blame reporting
4. **Measure:** reach, behaviour and outcome indicators
5. **Improve and sustain:** quarterly reviews, feedback, recognition and resource management
6. **Programme calendar:** month-by-month activities, owners and outputs
7. **Critical reflection:** design trade-offs and measurement limitations
